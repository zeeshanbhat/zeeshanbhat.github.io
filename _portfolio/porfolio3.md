I worked on **full-scale reversed out-of-plane cyclic testing** of **masonry infills** to investigate their **stability, cracking patterns, and failure mechanisms** under out-of-plane seismic-type loading.

**Key contributions:**
- Performed **full-scale out-of-plane reversed cyclic testing** of masonry infill panels  
- Studied the influence of **infill type and slenderness ratio** on out-of-plane response :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}  
- Evaluated **strength degradation, drift capacity, and collapse mechanisms** under cyclic loading  
- Supported performance assessment for **seismic safety and vulnerability evaluation**
