This paper investigated the seismic performance of reinforced concrete (RC) frame buildings designed for modern Indian seismic codes, with fly ash brick and autoclaved aerated concrete (AAC) block masonry infills. Multi-stripe dynamic analysis was performed on buildings with varying numbers of stories and three configurations: bare, fully infilled, and open-ground story frames. Fragility curves, annual collapse rates, and collapse margin ratios were evaluated. Results indicated that contrary to expectations based on pre- and low-code designs, open-ground story buildings designed for modern codes perform on par with their counterpart bare RC frame buildings, whereas fully infilled RC frame buildings outperform both.
