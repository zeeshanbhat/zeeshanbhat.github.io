I am a **Research Associate** in the **Department of Earthquake Engineering** at the **Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, India**.

My research focuses on the **seismic performance of masonry infilled reinforced concrete (RC) frame buildings**, combining **large-scale experimental testing** and **nonlinear modelling** for **seismic risk and vulnerability assessment**.

### Research Interests
- Large-scale experimental testing  
- Nonlinear modelling of structures  
- Seismic hazard, risk & vulnerability assessment  
- Performance-based earthquake engineering  
- Resilience-based seismic design
