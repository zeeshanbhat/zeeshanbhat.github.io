I am a Research Associate in the Department of Earthquake Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IIT Roorkee), India. My work combines advanced experimentation and computational modelling to improve the seismic safety and resilience of structural systems.

## Research Interests
- Large-scale experimental testing
- Nonlinear structural modelling
- Seismic hazard, risk, and vulnerability assessment
- Performance-based earthquake engineering
- Resilience-oriented seismic design
